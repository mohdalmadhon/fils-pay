# Audit finding 15: Manual credit fee uses current effective pricing

Validated against main `44c2b04`, 2026-10-30. Implemented on `future-fixes`.

## Confirmed problem

A credit used merchants.mdr_bps even when a currently applicable fee schedule changed that rate. A manual credit has no original transaction ID; exact historical charged-fee reversal cannot be inferred from its amount/date.

## Change

On issuance, fee reversal resolves the currently effective MDR, volume tier and negotiated discount with the established settlement formula. The computed amount remains frozen on the request for approval. The effective date remains the accounting date, not a false reconstruction of a payment. For exact payment refunds use the transaction-linked refund engine; historical credits are not automatically rewritten. No migration.

## Verification

node --import tsx scripts/future-fixes/15.verify.mjs creates fallback 245/current schedule 225 with negotiated discount and asserts the actual posted request fee follows the effective terms. The audit claim is narrowed to this reproducible pricing discrepancy, not a guarantee of historical reconstruction.
